# Pilot run, 2026-09-04 (kept for the record)

This is the first full run on Claude Code and Codex (Antigravity was a quarter of the way through), before one calibration pass of the ruleset. It is kept so the effect of that pass can be checked; the numbers in the top-level results file supersede it. The pilot report generated from these replies is next to this file.

What the pilot showed, and what changed because of it:

- Tenured caught 12 of 12 seeded defects on Claude Code, with 0 false alarms on the 4 clean diffs against 4 for the agent alone. Catch rate and discipline were not the problem.
- He gave `DO_NOT_REPEAT` to a warning comment, an ADR, a review the owners had rejected, and a metric name that once meant something else. Those are `SEEN_BEFORE` by his own definition, and `DO_NOT_REPEAT` precision was 50%. The ruleset now spells out the two things that earn `DO_NOT_REPEAT` (a recorded incident that names the failure, or a deliberate removal with a stated reason) and lists what does not, however strongly the record is worded.
- One case was on the wrong side of that line in the corpus: the re-added cleanup cron resurrects a job a commit deleted after an incident, which is a `DO_NOT_REPEAT` by the rule. Its expected class is now `BLOCK`.
- His replies ran to a median of 700 output tokens on Claude Code against 370 for the agent alone. Findings are now capped at five one-line items, each with its citation.

Raw replies for the pilot are in `raw/` next to this file. The persona arm was rerun on the revised rules; the other two arms did not change and were kept.
